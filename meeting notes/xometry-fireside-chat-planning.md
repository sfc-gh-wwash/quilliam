Here’s a concise summary of the discussion between Snowflake and Xometry about shaping a future fireside chat and exploring manufacturing data and AI opportunities.

### Overall Summary
The meeting centered on introducing **Greg Sloyer** to **Chandra** at Xometry and using that connection to plan a future fireside chat. The most important outcome was alignment on the chat’s purpose: balance external manufacturing and Snowflake trends with an informed discussion of Xometry’s roadmap, strengths, and future focus areas, with timing likely moved to the fall.

### Meeting Flow
- The call opened informally, then shifted into introductions. **Jamie Howell** framed the purpose as connecting **Chandra** with **Greg Sloyer**, Snowflake’s manufacturing industry principal, as a resource for a possible fireside chat and broader strategic discussion.
- **Chandra** introduced Xometry’s marketplace model: customers upload parts, Xometry generates instant quotes using proprietary ML, and work is then fulfilled through a partner manufacturing network. He emphasized that Xometry’s competitive advantage is not only its supplier network but also its proprietary data on pricing, lead times, manufacturing complexity, and supply across industries and geographies.
- From that context, **Chandra** asked for help in two areas: what manufacturing customers are doing with data, ML, and AI on Snowflake, and whether Snowflake’s ecosystem could help with data monetization or provide third-party data that would improve intelligence for Xometry’s customers or partners.
- **Greg Sloyer** shared his background across manufacturing, supply chain, logistics, analytics, and operations research, then highlighted a key industry theme: companies should not force AI/ML onto every problem when some use cases are better served by classic optimization techniques. He described this balance as increasingly important in manufacturing and logistics.
- The conversation then moved to practical ideas for Xometry. **Greg Sloyer** pointed to supply-chain risk visibility, dynamic pricing, commodities and freight data, and external datasets as relevant areas. **Chandra** noted that Xometry already does dynamic pricing and expressed particular interest in trends, marketplace apps, subscriptions, and accessible datasets such as commodities, logistics, and possibly materials-related signals.
- The final portion focused on the fireside chat format. **Chandra** said the session should cover both market trends and an informed outside view of Xometry’s roadmap—what the company is doing well and what it should prioritize next with Snowflake. The group agreed Snowflake would return with a proposed conversation flow, regroup in about a week, and aim for a fall timeframe when attendance would be stronger.

### Decisions Made
- Balance the fireside chat between manufacturing/Snowflake market trends and Xometry-specific roadmap discussion — agreed by **Chandra** and the Snowflake team.
- Target the fireside chat for the fall, likely **September**, rather than late July or early August — agreed by **Chandra** and the group.
