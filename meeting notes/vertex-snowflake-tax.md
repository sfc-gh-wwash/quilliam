The team aligned on exploring a Snowflake-Vertex tax motion, with EY positioned as a likely implementation partner, and the immediate focus is preparing a targeted outreach package for Vertex.

### Overall Summary
The discussion centered on whether Snowflake can turn tax into a repeatable transformation story by combining Snowflake’s data platform, EY’s tax and implementation expertise, and Vertex’s tax products. The clearest outcome was agreement to start with Vertex, frame the opportunity as complementary rather than competitive, and prepare materials to secure a meeting with the right Vertex stakeholders.

### Meeting Flow
- The presenter walked through a tax-focused go-to-market concept built from prior work with EY, including an accounting hub / tax data hub story. The pitch was that tax is often underserved, highly manual, financially material, and potentially a sticky Snowflake use case if implementation costs are kept reasonable.
- The group reviewed the core customer pain points: fragmented data, spreadsheet-heavy workflows, limited transparency, and the opportunity to reposition tax as a business enabler rather than only a back-office function. The presenter argued Snowflake can sit at the center because the required data can be consolidated there.
- The conversation then shifted to partner roles. EY was positioned as important because Snowflake does not want to take on tax-advisory risk directly; Snowflake brings the technology platform, while EY and similar firms bring tax expertise and implementation capacity.
- The team explored where Vertex fits. The working view was that Vertex should be treated as a “better together” partner whose products already create a natural cloud/tax entry point, while Snowflake expands the surrounding data and analytics use cases. They explicitly discussed avoiding overlap in areas Vertex wants to own, especially indirect tax.
- The latter part of the meeting focused on execution. The presenter asked for help identifying the right Vertex contact and relevant SI relationships, noted prior but limited momentum with KPMG, and committed to package the deck, highlight where Vertex plays today, and draft an email explaining why Vertex should take the meeting.

### Decisions Made
- [Decision] Start with **Vertex** as the first partner to pursue for this tax motion rather than broadening immediately to competitors.
- [Decision] Position Snowflake as the data platform and expansion layer, not as a competing tax product vendor.
- [Decision] Use firms such as **EY** for tax-specific implementation and risk-bearing work rather than having Snowflake engage as the tax expert.
