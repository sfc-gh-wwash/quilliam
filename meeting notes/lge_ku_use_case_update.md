# LG&E KU Use Case Update

Recommended Use Case Status: YELLOW

Partner POV:
Since the last update, momentum on the planned AI / agent use cases has slowed because the customer wants clearer governance, business value, and cost justification before moving forward with any agentic solutions. Near-term customer focus has shifted toward SAP-related priorities, reporting readiness, and overall platform cost posture rather than new feature deployment.

The larger blocker remains the AMI data warehouse environment. The existing use case was already tracking Qlik replication slowdowns and potential go-live risk, and the latest customer discussion indicates the AMI warehouse is still not reliably available for business use while recovery continues. Customer perception is that this is limiting the value they can realize from Snowflake today, which is also delaying adjacent AI / data quality workstreams discussed with the account team.

The opportunity is still strong, but success now depends on restoring confidence in the AMI foundation, clarifying ownership of recovery/remediation, and framing the proposed agent/data-quality work as both a business-value driver and a preventative control for future issues.

Next Steps:
* Re-escalate the AMI data warehouse recovery issue and available support options with Govind and Prasanjit.
* Build a clearer value and cost case for the proposed data-quality / agent solution before reintroducing it to customer leadership.
* Assess root-cause, monitoring, backup, and recovery approach with customer / partner stakeholders to reduce risk of repeat outages.
* Revisit enablement sessions and broader solution buildout once governance prerequisites are aligned and the AMI environment is stable.
